Frequently Asked Questions

How are Apex Outposts and Accessories delivered to my property? +
We offer white-glove flatbed delivery directly to your land, lease, or ranch. Outposts and feeders are built on heavy pressure-treated runner skids, allowing easy placement onto your food plot or gravel pad using a tractor, skid steer, UTV, or winch.
Can I customize options on individual tiers or accessories? +
To maintain strict quality control, rapid turnarounds, and 60% margin efficiency, our packages are standardized. If you require higher power, leather seating, or cedar interiors, simply step up to the Pro or Executive tier, or bundle matching accessories.
What is required to lock in a build slot? +
We require a 50% deposit to lock in your build queue position and secure raw materials. The remaining 50% balance is due upon completion prior to shipping or pickup.
